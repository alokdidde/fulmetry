## Current task (wave 1)

Implement MujocoSimulation (ISimulation on MuJoCo): load the rover MJCF, differential drive input, rover pose, telemetry and a deterministic state hash.

**Check:** MujocoSimulation passes SimulationConformance with rover-wheels-60.mjcf and rover-wheels-80.mjcf, or the stream doc explains which assertion real physics disagrees with.

## What is in app/sim

- `fulmetry_sim_mujoco` (static, only with `FULMETRY_WITH_MUJOCO=ON`): `fulmetry::sim::MujocoSimulation` and
  `makeMujocoSimulation()`. MuJoCo stays out of the public header. `fulmetry_sim` links it and defines
  `FULMETRY_HAS_MUJOCO=1` so the shell can pick it over the fake.
- MuJoCo is found with `-DMUJOCO_ROOT=<unpacked release>` (imported target) or, when that is empty,
  `find_package(mujoco CONFIG)` (vcpkg or source build, `mujoco::mujoco`).
- `loadModel` stages the MJCF text in a MuJoCo VFS and calls `mj_loadXML`; parse errors come back as
  `invalid_model` with MuJoCo's message. `reset(seed)` is `mj_resetData` + `mj_forward`.
- `setDriveInput`: wheel target = clamp(throttle ∓ steer, ±1) × max wheel speed into `drive_l` / `drive_r`
  (positive steer turns left). Max wheel speed is the motor's, the same for every wheel size: the upper
  `ctrlrange` of `drive_l` if the MJCF limits it, else 16.755 rad/s (160 rpm, 0.50 m/s on Ø60, matching the fake).
- `step(dt)`: whole model timesteps (2 ms); the remainder carries to the next call, so 60 × 1/60 s = 1.000 s.
- `bodyPose`: `xpos` × 1000 (mm) and `xquat` (w, x, y, z). Unknown body: `not_found`; no model: `not_loaded`.
- `telemetry`: `speed_mps` (signed, along the chassis +x), `on_rug` (any rover geom in contact with geom `rug`),
  `current_a` (0.15 A idle + |actuator torque| / 0.25 N·m/A + up to 0.08 A no-load per motor), `battery_pct`
  (current integrated against 2000 mAh), `rssi_dbm` (-48 dBm at ≤ 1 m from the origin, -20 dB/decade).
- `stateHash`: FNV-1a over seed, time, qpos, qvel, act (−0 folded to +0). Deterministic on one machine and
  MuJoCo build; not promised across platforms.

## Blockers

**For S10 (fixtures/rover): please adopt `app/sim/proposed/rover-wheels-60.mjcf` and `-80.mjcf`.** Three changes,
each a one-line diff:

1. **Rug height 8 mm → 3 mm** (`pos="0.9 0 0.0015" size="0.6 1 0.0015"`). On main the rug is a rigid 8 mm box,
   i.e. a curb. Both wheel sizes climb it, but the Ø16 mm caster ball (radius 8 mm) meets the edge at its own
   centre height, the contact normal is horizontal and the rover jams at x ≈ 355 mm with the wheels slipping.
   Whether and when it frees itself is chaotic (10 s runs): Ø60 is jammed for several seconds at throttle 1
   (354 mm at 4 s, 1074 mm at 10 s), never gets over at 0.99, 0.95 or 0.9, and gets over at once at 0.8; Ø80
   gets over after ~1.5 s at 1, 0.99, 0.95 and 0.9 and stays stuck at 0.8. So on main, the "bigger wheels" assertion and the
   1 m assertion pass by luck and could flip with a MuJoCo version, CPU or compiler change. A 3 mm box models the
   8 mm pile compressed under the rover; the caster then rolls over it every time.
2. **`integrator="implicitfast"`**. The wheels' velocity servos (kv 0.05 on a 8e-6 kg·m² wheel, time constant
   ~0.2 ms) are unstable with the default Euler integrator at 2 ms: MuJoCo reports NaN QACC within 20 ms.
   MujocoSimulation switches Euler models to implicitfast at load (option `preferImplicitIntegrator`), so this is
   not blocking, but the fixture should say it.
3. **`ctrlrange="-16.755 16.755"` on drive_l / drive_r**. Makes the motor's top speed (160 rpm) part of the model;
   MujocoSimulation uses it as the throttle = 1 wheel speed. Same value as the built-in default, so no behaviour
   change today.

**For the integrator / UI spec:** with real physics the "bigger wheels cross the rug faster" result is real, but
not for the reason the fake gives. The rug does not slow either wheel: it has more grip (μ 1.4), and the servos
have ample torque. Ø80 is 1.33× faster everywhere because the same 160 rpm motor turns a bigger circumference
(the fake instead slows Ø60 to 0.4× on the rug). The physical story in the main fixture is "the caster ball jams
on the rug edge", which bigger wheels do not fix (the caster is the same size). Making pile drag depend on wheel
size needs a soft-terrain model MuJoCo does not have; rolling friction (`condim="6"`) changed distances by < 2 %.
No conformance change is proposed: the 1.2× threshold holds at 1.33×.

## Decisions and findings

- MuJoCo 3.3.7 official release, unpacked at `~/deps/mujoco-3.3.7` (sha256 verified against the release's
  `.sha256`). The release has no CMake package, hence `MUJOCO_ROOT`. Not vendored; S10 decides vcpkg vs release.
- Drive maps throttle to wheel angular speed (one motor for all wheel sizes), not to ground speed: swapping
  wheels in the design should change the speed the way it would on the desk.
- The seed only enters the hash; nothing in the model is stochastic yet.
- MuJoCo prints its own warnings to stdout (e.g. instability). Not redirected yet.
