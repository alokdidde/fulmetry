Layout: a root `HBox` holds the stage `Overlay` and the 352 px chat column with a 1 px rule; "Dock left" (or
`--chat-left`) moves the chat to the other side. No `DockArea`, so there are no tab strips. Everything else
floats over the stage in the `Overlay`: top bar (brand, object tabs, Simulate), totals, Parts button and tray,
the pinned comment (Open, Send), the object or part card with Supported / Refuted / Unresolved pills and
reasons, the hint line, the Actions pill and its menu of icon tiles in groups.

**The S2 hand-over:** the stage is `fulmetry::ui::StageImage`. S2's offscreen Wicked render calls
`StageImage::setFrame(const fulmetry::Image&)` (RGBA8, the core type) each frame; it is drawn to cover the stage
under the panels. `frameToWidget()` maps a frame pixel to the stage, which the pinned comment uses; once S2
projects anchors, `WorkbenchView::setPinAnchor(part, x, y)` takes them instead of the JSON. Zero-copy (a Vulkan
image view through `Rvulkan_texture_create_from_image_view`) can replace the RGBA upload later without changing
the view.

### Matches the reference, and what does not yet

Matches: positions and sizes of every panel (within a few px of `workbench-highkey.png`), colours, 1 px lines,
3 px corners and soft shadows, Inter Tight / Inter / Plex Mono in the right weights, tracked upper-case
eyebrows, the tab underline, pills, chips with swatches, agent cards clamped to two lines, dividers, the About
chip and the red Send. Data is the fixture's (13 and 6 parts, $29.70, 356 g), so the numbers differ from the
prototype's.

Not yet (M1 integration made the stage live: orbit, pick, hover, outline, explode, x-ray, pin following); right-click on a part and the single-key shortcuts (C, I, H, X, J, P, S) are not wired (Escape is: menu, tray,
selection); agent threads, the isolate bar, full-stage sheets and simulation are not built; the menu's
actions other than Explode, X-ray, Reset view and All objects only print their name; text has no kerning and line height is Raven's (tighter than the prototype's 1.5).

### Live stage (M1 integration, integrator)

With `FULMETRY_WITH_WICKED` and `FULMETRY_WITH_RAVEN_GUI` (preset `ci-m1`) the stage is the live Wicked render of the
project; without Wicked, or with `--still`, it is the image above.

- `LiveStage` (`app/ui/raven`, IViewport only): drag orbits round the subject (elevation 4-86 deg), right- or
  middle-drag pans, the wheel zooms towards the cursor. Each change renders one quick frame (hard shadow); after 0.25 s
  at rest one 8-frame soft-shadow render replaces it. A click picks a part and selects it in the model (part card, tray
  row); a click on nothing clears the selection; a resting cursor hovers the part under it. The model drives the
  viewport: object tab (refits), selection outline, explode and x-ray. It prints `stage-render`, `stage-settled`,
  `stage-click`, `stage-hover` and `part-at` lines for scripts.
- Framing: `WorkbenchView::freeStageRect()` (stage minus top bar, Parts, inspector, Actions and hint) becomes the
  viewport's framing rectangle, so the subject is never under a panel when a tab opens.
- The pinned comment follows its anchor (`Anchor.point` in the object frame, riding with explode and the All objects
  layout) through `IViewport::project`: `StageImage` draws a ringed dot and a leader; the card sits beside or above the
  subject (shorter leader wins), never under a panel, and hides when its part is not shown.
- `WorkbenchModel` has `exploded` and `xray`; the Actions menu toggles them (active tiles tinted, Explode reads
  Assemble), has an X-ray tile, and Reset view refits.
- Flags: `--tab N --explode --xray --orbit DX,DY --zoom N --hover <part> --still --interactive-scale S`; `--shot` waits
  for the resting render.
- Screenshots: `app/ui/shots/m1/` (`rover`, `camera-head`, `exploded`, `all-objects`, `actions-menu`, `interaction`),
  from `app/ui/tests/m1_shots.sh`.

### Simulate (drive test)

Simulate (the button, the menu tile, or `--simulate [setup.json]`) puts the object in a room and drives it:
`fixtures/rover/sim/living-room.json` names the MJCF model, which parts ride on which MJCF body, the floor, the props
drawn on the stage (`IViewport::setEnvironment`) and a scripted route. `DriveTest` (`app/ui`, GUI-free, tested with
FakeSimulation and FakeViewport) steps the `ISimulation`, poses the parts (`IViewport::setPartPose`) and keeps the
telemetry and findings; `SimulateMode` (`app/ui/raven`) shows its card in place of the inspector, follows the rover
with the camera and reads W/S/A/D, R and Esc. With `FULMETRY_WITH_MUJOCO` (preset `ci-m1`) the physics is MuJoCo,
otherwise the kinematic fake, which slows 60 mm wheels on the rug where MuJoCo does not: findings report what the
physics measured. `--sim-seconds T` steps to T at 60 Hz and holds (screenshots), `--sim-drive THROTTLE,STEER` drives
by hand. `app/ui/tests/sim_shots.sh` takes three screenshots and checks W and Esc in a real window.

### Raven limitations met

- No letter spacing: eyebrows and the brand get hair spaces (U+200A) between letters (`kit::tracked`).
- No line clamp: `kit::clampLines` wraps with `Rfont_measure_text` and cuts with an ellipsis.
- No line height or kerning control for labels.
- A `Border` has one outline for all sides, so the pinned card's left accent bar, the tab underline and the
  chat's dividing line are separate 3, 2 and 1 px widgets.
- A `Border` is not a button: clicks and hover go through `Rwidget_set_event_callback` (`kit::onClick`); work
  that rebuilds widgets is queued and run after the frame, never inside the callback.
- `Shadow` takes blur and offset; its colour is the theme's `Shadow` role for every panel.
- Single-letter `<Shortcut>`s would fire while typing in the composer, so they are not registered yet.
- Rebuilding a list means `Rwidget_clear_children` and building it again (fine for tens of rows; the chat
  will need recycling for long histories, as found in wave 1).

On lavapipe (CPU rasteriser) the full scene ran **18.9 fps at 1440 x 900** (mean 52.8 ms, p50 46.9, p95 99.4, p99 115.0, max 168.5 ms over 600 frames), **62 fps at 720 x 450**, and the UI alone (`--empty-stage`) **95 fps at 1440 x 900** (mean 10.5 ms). ## Other findings

- **API maturity: low.** v0.2.2; the first commit was 2026-10-09 (four weeks ago); 231 commits, ~90 % by
  one author; the README says "public API is subject to change". It is moving fast (tags 0.1.2 to 0.2.2 in
  a month) and the code quality is good: C11, opaque generation-checked handles, a render thread, a job
  system, tests clean under ASan/UBSan/TSan, a stable `RInitDesc` that takes new fields. It built first
  time with Ninja, and the spike compiled against its headers with no warnings under our C++20
  `-Wall -Wextra -Wpedantic -Werror` (Raven's own C is built with `-w`). Risk is bus factor
  and churn, not code quality. Pin the commit and wrap it behind our own panel API in `app/ui`.
- **Docs: good for a project this young.** A 70 KB README with a section per feature, `docs/XML.md`
  (every tag and attribute), `THEMES.md`, `ARCHITECTURE.md`, an HTML reference, a Qt-to-Raven guide, and
  headers with a comment on almost every function. The English is idiosyncratic but exact. No
  versioned API reference or changelog per release apart from `ROADMAP.md`.
- **XML and live reload:** worked as described. The `<Stage>` tag came from `Rxml_register_tag`; widgets are
  found by `Name` (`Rwidget_find`); the reload keeps handles, callbacks and typed text of elements that did
  not change, and `Rxml_set_reload_callback` tells us when to look widgets up again.
- **Text, fonts, shaping:** glyphs come from `stb_truetype` with an atlas and subpixel positioning; bidi and
  Arabic joining exist, but there is **no OpenType shaping and no kerning** (README "Not implemented":
  kerning, Indic scripts). **Inter Tight** (Google Fonts variable TTF) and **IBM Plex Mono** loaded with
  `Rfont_create_from_file` and rendered cleanly (screenshot: UI in Inter Tight, the stage caption in Plex
  Mono). A variable font is drawn at its default instance only (no `wght` axis); bold, medium and semibold
  need static TTFs per weight via `Rfont_set_variant`. Fallback fonts and colour emoji come from the system.
- **DPI scaling: works.** With `Xft.dpi: 192` (xrdb) the window reported scale 2.00 and the whole UI laid
  out at 2x with sharp text (checked on a crop). Layout is in logical units throughout. Wayland fractional
  scaling is supported; Windows/macOS per-monitor scaling is claimed but not tested here.
- **Theming to our spec:** `R_LOOK_RAVEN_LIGHT` + `Rtheme_set_accent(red)` + `R_METRIC_CORNER_RADIUS 3` gave
  the high-key white, 3 px, red-accent look with a few calls; per-widget overrides in XML (`CornerRadius`,
  `Background`, `Shadow`).
- **Wicked Engine viewport:** three ways in, by preference:
  1. *Shared device, zero copy*: `Rvulkan_set_external_device(instance, physical, device, queue, family)`
     before `Rinit`, plus `Rvulkan_set_queue_lock_callbacks`, and Wicked's render target shown with
     `Rvulkan_texture_create_from_image_view`. The UI then floats over the stage as the spec needs. Cost:
     Wicked creates its own `VkInstance`/`VkDevice` internally, so S2 must patch or wrap
     `GraphicsDevice_Vulkan` to enable Raven's required extensions
     (`Rvulkan_get_required_instance_extensions`/`_device_extensions`), expose its handles, and take the
     queue lock around its own `vkQueueSubmit`/`vkQueuePresentKHR`. Ordering is by submission on the one
     queue; Raven exposes no semaphores.
  2. *Two devices, shared memory*: `Rvulkan_texture_create_shared` exports an image as an opaque fd that
     Wicked imports. No Wicked device change, but no cross-device sync is exposed (we would see tearing or
     need a fence round-trip on the CPU).
  3. *Native child window* (`Rviewport_set_native`): works with stock Wicked, but Raven "cannot draw over
     or clip that area", so floating panels over the stage are impossible. Not acceptable for this UI.
  So: the device can be shared, but only with a Wicked-side patch. Option 1 is the plan; it needs a spike
  in S2.
- **Headless testing:** works well. Xvfb + lavapipe + xdotool drive the real X11 path. Raven also has a
  null platform and a null backend (`RInitDesc.headless`, `R_BACKEND_NULL`) for unit tests without a
  display. One trap: start Xvfb with `-noreset`, or root properties such as `Xft.dpi` vanish when the last
  client disconnects.

## Recommendation

**Keep Raven GUI.** All four criteria pass on this machine: docking, text input and the scrolling chat
work as specified, and custom drawing is cheap on the CPU side (0.14 ms per frame). The 60 fps result is
provisional until we measure on a GPU. Raven gives us what Qt 6 would cost much more to get: a Vulkan renderer we can
point at Wicked's device (no QRhi/QVulkanWindow interop), one C header and no runtime or licence weight
(Apache-2.0, ~1.3 MB, no moc), XML with live reload for fast iteration on the UI spec, and good
theming for the high-key look.

Conditions, any of which flips the call to Qt 6:

1. Wicked must share its Vulkan device (option 1 above). If S2 cannot patch Wicked for that, the only
   stock path is a native child window, which breaks "everything floats over the stage". Qt would face the
   same problem; this is about Wicked. But it removes Raven's main advantage.
2. 60 fps on a mid-range GPU (an integrated Intel/AMD at 1440p with the chat full and the stage
   animating) must be confirmed with `run_headless.sh` on real hardware in wave 2.
3. Text quality: no kerning or OpenType shaping. Acceptable for an English-first tool at UI sizes; if
   the design needs kerned display type or Indic scripts, we either contribute HarfBuzz shaping upstream or
   switch.
4. Upstream risk: one main author, four weeks old, API not frozen. We pin the commit, keep Raven
   behind `app/ui`'s own panel API (no `Rid` outside `app/ui`), and keep the option to vendor a fork.

## Blockers

- **S10 (root build):** for the `full` preset, Raven needs to come from somewhere other than a manual
  `RAVEN_GUI_ROOT`: either a vcpkg overlay port pinned to `a472444…` or a `FetchContent` in the root
  CMake. `app/ui/CMakeLists.txt` takes `RAVEN_GUI_ROOT` today; it can switch to `find_package(raven)`
  once S10 provides one. Raven is C: the root `project()` stays `LANGUAGES CXX`; Raven's own `project()`
  enables C (works with CMake 3.28).
- **S10 (CI):** a Linux job with Raven ON (once Raven comes from the build, above) that runs
  `ctest` (includes `ui_workbench_headless`, which needs no display or GPU: null platform and backend), and,
  with `xvfb mesa-vulkan-drivers xdotool imagemagick` installed, `app/ui/tests/shots.sh
  build/.../fulmetry_workbench shots/` with the PNGs uploaded as artefacts. The script fails on a broken
  click, tab or Send path. Nothing else at the root is needed: fonts, icons, the stage image and the XML live
  in `app/ui/assets` and the binary finds them through a compile definition (`FULMETRY_UI_ASSETS`); a packaged
  build will need them installed next to the binary (`--assets`).
- **S2 (viewport):** Wicked device sharing as above. Needs `GraphicsDevice_Vulkan` to accept extra
  instance and device extensions, expose `VkInstance/VkPhysicalDevice/VkDevice/VkQueue`, and lock its queue
  around submits.

## Decisions and findings

- 2026-11-07: Raven GUI kept for the workbench (spike above). Pinned at `a472444469dd46890e33585e81641309e591fa30`.
- The chat width (352 px) must be enforced by us: `DockSize` is a fraction.
- The stage will not be drawn with `Rdraw_*`; it is a Wicked texture in a `Viewport` widget. `Rdraw_*` is
  for overlays (selection outlines, gizmo labels) and should stay light: each line costs its bounding box
  in fragments.
- Fonts: ship static Inter Tight TTFs (Regular, Medium, SemiBold) and IBM Plex Mono Regular; register the
  weights with `Rfont_set_variant`.
- M1 shell: no `DockArea` (its tab strips are not in the spec); an `HBox` of stage and chat, and the chat side
  is swapped by moving the two widgets. The chat is 352 px fixed, so the fraction problem is gone.
- M1 shell: the GUI-free `WorkbenchModel` is in `fulmetry_ui` and tested in the default build; Raven types stay
  in `fulmetry_ui_raven`. The shell (`app/shell`, integrator) can link either.
- M1 shell: until S2 renders offscreen, the stage is a still of the prototype's own rover (the wave 1 Wicked
  render was a box with a cube, which Alok found too bare).
